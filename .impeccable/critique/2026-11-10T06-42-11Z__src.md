### Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict: Highly Authored & Distinctive (Thermal Ledger Aesthetic)**

- **LLM Assessment**: AsingAsing rejects generic fintech and SaaS templates in favor of a bespoke **"Thermal Ledger"** aesthetic. The combination of warm washi paper (`#FAF7F2`), crisp sumi charcoal ink (`#1C1917`), 1px crease borders (`#E5DDCF`), Newsreader editorial serif headings, and Geist Mono tabular numerals feels physical, deliberate, and mathematically honest. It accurately captures the real-world conventions of Malaysian dining: base food 10% Service Charge, Malaysian SST compounded on subtotal + service charge, 5-cent cash rounding, and single-payer cent allocation. Minor cultural hybridity remains from its codebase evolution (e.g. Japanese placeholder dishes like `Ebiten Ebikko Maki` in an `RM` ledger, and legacy `betsubetsu` keys in localStorage), but the core experience is distinctly grounded.
- **Deterministic Scan (`impeccable detect`)**:

  - **1 genuine token drift**: `src/components/ocr/ReceiptCapture.tsx:456` uses an off-ramp utility `text-[11px]` instead of standard `text-xs` (12px).

### Overall Impression

AsingAsing is an exceptionally crafted utility with a rare combination of mathematical rigor, visual tactility, and cultural relevance. Its core mechanism—proportional tax and service charge distribution paired with single-payer cent closure—solves the genuine social pain of dining bill settlement without feeling like an accounting spreadsheet. The single biggest opportunity is decluttering the initial image capture state and reducing tap-fatigue during multi-person dish assignment on mobile.

---

### What's Working

1. **Tactile Paper Slips & Monospace Tabular Precision:** The interface looks and feels like examining a crisp restaurant receipt. Using monospace tabular figures for all monetary amounts ensures columns align perfectly, conveying immediate accounting trust.
2. **Mathematically Honest Proportional Math Engine:** Solving the unfairness of equal tax splitting (where someone ordering water subsidizes someone ordering steak) through automated proportional weighting and cent-balanced rounding closure is executed brilliantly.
3. **Frictionless Social Handoff & The Rounding Roulette:** Exporting an instant, clean WhatsApp/Telegram breakdown with optional payment details (e.g. DuitNow/QR) completes the settlement loop in seconds. Transforming the awkward "who pays the odd 3 cents" dilemma into a lighthearted roulette wheel with celebratory confetti turns accounting friction into social delight.

### Priority Issues (P0–P3)

#### [P1] Issue 1: Visual Token Divergence from Design System
- **Why it matters**: In `ReceiptCapture.tsx`, `ReceiptEditor.tsx`, and `ParticipantMatrix.tsx`, status alerts and badges fall back to generic Tailwind utilities (`bg-amber-50`, `border-amber-300`, `text-emerald-900`) and arbitrary font sizes (`text-[11px]`). This violates the authored thermal paper rules in `DESIGN.md` and softens the brand identity into generic web software.
- **Fix**: Standardize all banners to use Washi paper backgrounds with `#C53030` (Hanko Vermilion) for discrepancies/unassigned alerts, `#1B6B38` (Matcha Seal) for balanced confirmations, and replace `text-[11px]` with `text-xs`.
- **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable polish`

#### [P1] Issue 2: Choice Overload and Technical Jargon on Receipt Capture
- **Why it matters**: After uploading an image, `ReceiptCapture.tsx` presents 6 competing actions and exposes deep image-processing jargon ("Thermal Ink Binarization", threshold sliders, contrast filters) alongside API settings. In a dimly lit, noisy restaurant, this induces cognitive friction and hesitation.
- **Fix**: Keep "Scan with AI" as the undisputed hero action. Consolidate offline Tesseract OCR, tuning sliders, and manual text paste under a collapsed progressive disclosure disclosure labeled *"Offline or Manual Options"*.
- **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable layout`

#### [P2] Issue 3: High Tap Friction for Batch Item Assignment on Mobile
- **Why it matters**: On a typical 15-dish group meal with 5 friends, assigning dishes requires 40 to 60 individual screen taps. While "Split All" exists, common shared subgroups (e.g., dishes shared by Person 1 and Person 2) have to be manually retapped on every row.
- **Fix**: Add a *"Repeat Split from Above"* one-tap chip on line-item rows, allowing organizers to duplicate the previous item's assignment instantly.
- **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable adapt`

#### [P2] Issue 4: Destructive Bill Reset via Unstyled `window.confirm`
- **Why it matters**: Tapping the reset icon in `Header.tsx:63` fires the browser's jarring native modal `window.confirm()`. On mobile Safari/Chrome, this disrupts the immersive tactile flow and offers no undo safety net if tapped accidentally.
- **Fix**: Replace native dialog with an inline paper modal confirmation or an automatic 5-second transient "Bill cleared • [Undo]" toast.
- **Suggested Command**: `/impeccable harden`
